What was changed: every form the hunspell dictionary accepts was generated
(matrix-game-next-gen, Scripts/build-language.py), capitalised entries were
dropped, forms of 2 to 15 letters without apostrophes were kept, Zipf
frequencies from wordfreq were attached, and the result was compiled into
Words.lex.gz (2,746,623 forms). That compiled list is distributed under the
same terms as its source, and the game's page carries this attribution.
